Regulation of Blood Glucose by Insulin-Glucagon

When Blood Glucose is High:

  • Pancreas releases insulin hormone into the bloodstream.
  • Insulin increases glucose absorption by body tissues and converts excess glucose into glycogen in the liver.
  • This brings blood glucose level to normal.

When Blood Glucose is Low:

  • Pancreas releases glucagon hormone into the bloodstream.
  • Glucagon triggers the liver to break down glycogen into glucose, which enters the blood.
  • This brings blood glucose level to normal.
