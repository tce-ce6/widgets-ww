If two triangles are similar, their corresponding sides are in proportion, and their corresponding angles are equal.

There are three criteria to prove that two triangles are similar:

🔹 AA (Angle-Angle) Similarity Criterion
If two angles of one triangle are equal to two angles of another triangle, then the triangles are similar.
✅ △ABC ∼ △DEF
(∠A = ∠D and ∠B = ∠E)
