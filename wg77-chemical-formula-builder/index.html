Rules for Writing Chemical Formulae
  • Write the symbol of the cation first and the anion next.
  • Write the charge (valency) of each ion.
  • Cross the charge of one ion to become the subscript of the other.
  • Divide the subscripts by their common factor to get the simplest ratio.
  • A polyatomic ion (SO42-, OH-, NO3-, CO32-, NH4+) is a group of atoms that acts as one unit. If more than one is needed, put it in brackets, e.g. Ca(OH)2. Never change the numbers inside the ion.
  • A subscript 1 is not written.
