How is the Median Calculated?

  1. Arrange the data points in order from smallest to largest.
  2. If there’s an odd number of points, the middle value is the median.
  3. If there’s an even number of points, the median is the average of the two middle values.

You can drag the dots vertically to change the values and observe how the median updates dynamically.
