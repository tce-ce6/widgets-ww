Triangles Similar or Not

Based on the markings, determine if △ABC ⁓ △DEF.

Two triangles △ABC and △DEF are similar (△ABC ∼ △DEF)

  1. If two angles of one triangle are equal to two angles of another triangle, the triangles are similar.

    △ABC ∼ △DEF implies:

    • AB / DE = BC / EF = AC / DF
  2. If the three sides of one triangle are in the same ratio as the three sides of another triangle, then the triangles are similar.

    △ABC ∼ △DEF implies:

    • AB / DE = BC / EF = AC / DF
  3. If two sides of one triangle are in the same ratio as two sides of another triangle, and the included angles are equal, the triangles are similar.

    △ABC ∼ △DEF implies:

    • AB / DE = BC / EF and ∠A = ∠D
