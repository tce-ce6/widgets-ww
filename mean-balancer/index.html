The data shows the math grades of  10 Students. Plot the points and then balance them to find the mean.
Number of data points   10

How is the Mean Calculated?

  1. Add all the numbers together.
      This gives you the sum of the values.
  2. Count how many numbers there are.
      This gives you the number of values.
  3. Divide the sum by the number of values.
      This gives you the mean.

Formula:

Mean = (Sum of all values) / (Number of values)

You can drag the balancer horizontally to check the values and observe how the mean updates dynamically.
