​Population Growth with Unlimited and Limited Resources​

b4_wg224_261117_01

Choose a type of resource condition to explore how a population grows.

Unlimited Resources

dN/dt = rN

Food and space never run out.
The population keeps growing without any ceiling.

Limited Resources

dN/dt = rN(K−N)/K

The habitat can only support a fixed number.
Growth slows as the population fills up.

Unlimited Resources

N0 — Starting population

How many individuals are there at the beginning?

r — Growth rate

Limited Resources

N0 — Starting population

How many individuals are there at the beginning?

At least 1, and less than K

r — Growth rate

How fast can the population grow
when resources are unlimited?

Between 0.01 and 2

K — Carrying capacity

The maximum population the habitat can support.

Between 20 and 5000; must be greater than N0

Numbers are locked while stepping. Tap Reset to change them.

dN/dt = rN(K−N)/K

dN/dt = rN(K−N)/K

How this year’s value is calculated: Nt+1 = Nt + r × Nt × (K−Nt)/K
