What You Saw

🌊

Two rivers — the Huang He and Yangtze — fed villages that became cities by 1600 BCE.

🔮

Our earliest record of China is oracle bones: cracked shells carrying everyone’s worries, not just the king’s.

🥈

Four dynasties — Shang, Zhou, Qin, Han — mastered bronze, jade and marble.

🧵

Silk became China’s great export, and the road it travelled took its name.
