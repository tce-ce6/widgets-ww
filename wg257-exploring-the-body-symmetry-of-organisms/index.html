Body Symmetry in Organisms

  • Central axis: An imaginary line passing through the centre of the body.
  • Plane of dissection: An imaginary flat surface that divides the body into two parts.
  • Identical halves: The two halves are mirror images of each other.
  • Radial symmetry: The body can be divided into two identical halves along more than one plane passing through the central axis.
  • Bilateral symmetry: The body can be divided into two identical halves along only one plane (the sagittal plane) passing through the central axis.
  • Asymmetry: The body cannot be divided into two identical halves along any plane.
  • Note: Some animals with a bilateral body plan look asymmetrical from outside, because one part of the body grows unevenly.
