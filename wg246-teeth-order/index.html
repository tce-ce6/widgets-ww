  • Humans have heterodont teeth, which means the teeth differ in shape and do different jobs.
  • Incisors are chisel-shaped and used for biting and cutting.
  • Canines are pointed and used for holding and tearing food.
  • Premolars have two cusps (bicuspid), and molars have a broad surface. Both grind and crush food.
  • The last molar on each side of each jaw is the wisdom tooth, which appears at about 17 to 20 years.
  • Milk teeth (temporary or deciduous) grow from about 7 to 8 months and are replaced by permanent teeth by about 12 years.
  • The dental formula shows the teeth in one half of each jaw in the order i, c, pm, m.
  • Child: 2, 1, 0, 22, 1, 0, 2 = 20 milk teeth (no premolars)
  • Adult: 2, 1, 2, 32, 1, 2, 3 = 32 permanent teeth
