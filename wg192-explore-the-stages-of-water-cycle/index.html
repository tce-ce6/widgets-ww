  • The water cycle is the continuous movement of water between the waterbodies, the atmosphere and the land, driven by energy from the Sun.
  • Evaporation: It is the transformation of water from liquid to vapour at the surface of seas, oceans, rivers, soil and plant leaves, using heat from the Sun.
  • Condensation: It is the transformation of water vapour back into tiny liquid droplets when rising air cools, and these droplets collect to form clouds.
  • Precipitation: It is the fall of water from clouds to the Earth's surface as rain, snow, sleet or hail, once the droplets become too heavy for the air to hold.
  • Collection: It is the gathering of fallen water in oceans, rivers, lakes and underground stores, from where it evaporates again.
  • Water vapour also forms through transpiration from the leaves of plants.
  • Water that soaks into the ground becomes groundwater and reaches rivers slowly, so it is delayed rather than lost.
