Between Lines - Transversal Geometry

Lines are NOT PARALLEL
Highlight Mode: NONE

Instructions:

  • Drag the endpoints of lines to change their slope and position
  • Drag anywhere on the transversal to move it
  • Use buttons to highlight different angle relationships
  • Toggle between parallel and non-parallel lines
  • Observe how angle relationships change

Angle Relationships:

  • Corresponding Angles: Equal when lines are parallel (e.g., ∠A = ∠E)
  • Alternate Interior Angles: Equal when lines are parallel (e.g., ∠C = ∠F)
  • Co-Interior Angles: Sum to 180° when lines are parallel (e.g., ∠C + ∠E = 180°)
