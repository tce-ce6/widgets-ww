Commonly Confused Words
TP ID


Learning Objective
Distinguish between commonly confused words based on pronunciation differences.
Brief description
Students learn the pronunciation difference between commonly confused word pairs, then hear a word in context and choose the correct spelling from two similar-looking options.

Widget Title
Commonly Confused Words
I-text
Unlock the Hidden Picture! (Listen carefully and choose the correct word)

Functionality
Wireframe

Screen 1: Game Screen (Initial State)
Visual Elements:
	•	Widget title at the top
	•	Progress bar showing: 'Pieces collected: 0/36' and 'Question 1/36'
	•	Puzzle preview grid (6 columns and 6 rows) showing 36 empty grey squares
	•	Main game card with: 
	•	'Listen to the Sentence' button 
	•	Two word display boxes showing the current word pair. Disabled until the sentence audio is played fully.
	•	Question text: Tap the word you heard in the sentence.'

The I-text ‘Tap multiple times to hear again’ is not needed. Also replace ‘Which word did you hear’ with ‘Tap the word you heard in the sentence.’

Functionality: 
	•	Initially only ‘Listen to the Sentence’ button will be active.
	•	After the sentence audio is played. The word buttons will be active. 
	•	Once the user taps the correct box, the audio for the word will play.
	•	One image from the puzzle grid will be revealed. Make sure the images are not revealed in sequence.

	•	The progress bar will show 1/36.

	•	If the wrong box is tapped, show animation to indicate it’s the wrong answer. 

	•	The user tries again. 

	•	When user taps on ‘Next’, screen 2 opens with new word pair. 

The cycle repeats until all sentences are played and all puzzle pieces are revealed.


Note:
Each word is linked to a sentence demonstrating its usage. Every word pair (e.g., trail/trial) appears twice in the widget, but the two instances are separated by other word pairs to ensure the correct answer is not obvious.

Data Table
	•	  trail /trial
	•	We followed the mountain trail to reach the waterfall.
	•	The judge will begin the trial tomorrow morning.

	•	accept/except
	•	Prasad was happy to accept the prize for the drawing competition.
	•	Everyone came to school today except Rashid.

	•	desert/dessert
	•	Camels can survive in the hot desert without water for days.
	•	Mum made chocolate cake for dessert after dinner.

	•	breath/breathe
	•	Take a deep breath before you start singing.
	•	It's difficult to breathe when you have a bad cold.

	•	from/form
	•	I walked home from school with my friends.
	•	We will form a circle and play a game.

	•	quiet/quite
	•	Please be quiet in the library so others can read.
	•	The maths exam was quite difficult this time.

	•	dairy/diary
	•	Milk, cheese and yoghurt are all dairy products.
	•	I write about my day in my diary every night.

	•	angel/angle
	•	The Christmas tree had a beautiful angel on top.
	•	Use your protractor to measure the angle between these two lines.

	•	collar/colour
	•	The dog wore a red collar with a bell on it.
	•	My favourite colour is bright blue.
	•	later/latter
	•	We can finish our homework later in the evening.
	•	I like both mangoes and oranges, but prefer the latter.

	•	moral/morale
	•	The moral of the story is to always tell the truth.
	•	The team's morale was high after winning the match.

	•	affect/effect
	•	Lack of sleep can affect your ability to concentrate.
	•	The medicine had a positive effect on my headache.

	•	adopt/adapt
	•	My family decided to adopt a puppy from the shelter.
	•	Plants adapt to their environment to survive.

	•	then/than
	•	I brushed my teeth, and then I went to bed.
	•	My sister is taller than me. 
	•	sweet/sweat
	•	The mangoes from our garden taste very sweet.
	•	After playing football in the sun, I began to sweat. 
	•	breath/breathe
	•	I took a deep breath before diving into the pool.
	•	It is difficult to breathe when you have a cold.

	•	decent/descent

	•	Wear decent clothes when you visit the temple with your family. 
	•	The aeroplane began its descent towards Mumbai airport.

	•	through/thorough
	•	We walked through the garden to reach the playground.
	•	The teacher did a thorough check of all our homework copies.
