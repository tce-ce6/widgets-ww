Your first day as manager

Welcome to Happy Sports Workshop. You have 6 workers and a fixed set of machines. That is all: these are your resources. Each day the workshop can make cricket bats, hockey sticks, or a mix.

The curve. The green line on your planning chart shows every maximum combination of bats and sticks the workshop can make in a day. It is your boundary line.

Opportunity cost. Resources are limited, so making one more bat means pulling workers away from sticks. The sticks given up are the cost of that bat, paid in sticks, not money.

Three kinds of plans. On the line: efficient, nothing wasted. Inside the line: inefficient, something sits idle. Outside the line: unattainable, beyond your resources.

Your workshop's full menu. This schedule lists every maximum daily plan. Each column is one point on the curve, from A to F.

How to play

1. Drag the orange dot on the chart to set the day's production plan. On a keyboard, press Tab to reach the dot, move it with the arrow keys, and press Enter to lock the plan.

2. Release the dot to lock the plan in. The workshop will report back.

3. Some missions ask you to answer before you move. Read each work order carefully.

4. Complete all 4 missions to earn your Manager's badge.
