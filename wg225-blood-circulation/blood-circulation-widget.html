Blood Circulation

Blood travels through the body, heart and lungs in a continuous pathway. This pathway is formed by different blood vessels and sections of the heart.

Flags

    • Blood that needs oxygen
    • Blood full of oxygen

    The heart is drawn facing you, so its right side is on your left.

    Insights

    Body → Right atrium → Right ventricle → Lungs → Left atrium → Left ventricle → Body

    • Blood moves around the body in a loop, always in the same direction.
    • Blood from the body needs oxygen. It enters the heart at the right atrium.
    • It moves down to the right ventricle, which pumps it to the lungs.
    • In the lungs, blood takes in oxygen and gives away carbon dioxide.
    • Blood full of oxygen comes back to the left atrium, then moves down to the left ventricle.
    • The left ventricle is the strongest part of the heart. It pumps blood to every part of the body.
    • In one trip, blood passes through the heart twice: first the right side, then the left side.
    • Blue shows blood that needs oxygen. Red shows blood full of oxygen.
