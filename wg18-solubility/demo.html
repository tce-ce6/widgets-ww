Core Concepts

🧪 Solute

The substance being dissolved (e.g., sugar). It breaks into particles that spread through the solvent.

💧 Solvent

The dissolving medium (e.g., water). "Like dissolves like" — polar solvents dissolve polar solutes.

⚡ Saturation

When no more solute can dissolve at a given temperature. Excess solute remains as a solid precipitate.

🌡️ Temperature Effect

For most solids, higher temperature → higher solubility. For gases, the opposite is true.

📊 Concentration

Mass of solute per volume of solution. More solute or less solvent = higher concentration.
