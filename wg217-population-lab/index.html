Insight: the three stages

The birth rate is the number of babies born in a year for every 1,000 people. The death rate is the number of people who die in a year for every 1,000 people. As these two rates change, a country's population moves through three stages.

Stage1
High birth rate, high death rate

Many babies are born, but many people also die. The population grows very slowly.

Stage2
Death rate falls first, then birth rate

Better healthcare helps people live longer, so deaths fall first. Families stay large for a while and then slowly get smaller. The population grows fast.

Stage3
Birth rate and death rate are both low

The two rates come close together. The population grows very slowly and may even start to shrink.
